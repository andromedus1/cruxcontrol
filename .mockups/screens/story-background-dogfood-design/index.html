CruxControl / Ambient motion study / Dogfood round two

Small lights, clear motion.

Two new Matrix directions and four possible backgrounds. Each uses ten or fewer effect holds on the actual Fullride layout. Every pose lasts half a second. These are design studies; they do not change your climbs or control your board.

120-second ambient sequence. Play is opt-in; reduced motion pauses playback and keeps frame stepping available. Climb holds stay fixed and exclude the effect at those positions.

Colors and brightness are illustrative. The board's limited color steps and protection of climb-role colors can change the physical result. New effects would retain editable palettes.
