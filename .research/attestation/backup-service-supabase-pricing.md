# Supabase plan comparison

## Free and Pro plans

Free projects include 1 GB file storage and may pause after one inactive week. Pro starts at USD 25 per month, includes 100 GB file storage, and avoids free-project pausing. The price is a plan starting point with separate usage and add-on considerations, not a universal upper bound on all charges.
