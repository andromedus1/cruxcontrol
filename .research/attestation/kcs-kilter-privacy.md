# Kilter Privacy Policy

## Summary
Kilter, LLC (Boulder, CO); effective March 9, 2026. Collects names, addresses, email, phone, payment data via processors, usage data, browser/device identifiers. Shares with service providers (payment, hosting, analytics), online tool providers (e.g. Google Analytics, Shopify), advertisers; may transfer on sale/bankruptcy.

## Key passages
- EU residents: right "to request that we transfer all your Personal Information to another controller in a reasonably understandable format."
- Other users: data access on request to management@kiltergrips.com; deletion may prevent continued service use.

## Absences
No mention of public climbs, APIs, or the Aurora data transfer (per tool). Personal-data portability is the only export-like right described.
