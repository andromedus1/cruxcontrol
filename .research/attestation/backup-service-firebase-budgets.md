# Firebase budget controls

## Alerts-only budgets

Budget alerts send notifications at thresholds and do not pause services. The Blaze upgrade flow prompts for this type of budget.

## Spend cap budgets

The listed spend-cap support covers Firebase AI Logic, App Hosting, Cloud Functions, and Extensions. Cloud Storage is absent from that supported list. For supported services, the cap pauses the service for the remainder of the month when its budget reaches 100 percent.
