# Google Cloud free usage limits

## Cloud Storage

The monthly allowance includes 5 GB-months of regional storage, 5,000 Class A operations, 50,000 Class B operations, and 100 GB outbound transfer from North America excluding China and Australia. Storage benefits apply only in `us-east1`, `us-west1`, and `us-central1`, combined across those regions.

## Free Tier usage limits

An active billing account is required. Limits apply per billing account, and usage beyond the allowances is billed. These limits are distinct from the temporary Free Trial. The program can change; it is not an unconditional promise of permanently free storage.
