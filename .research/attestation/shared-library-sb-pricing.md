# Supabase — Pricing & Fees

## Summary

Supabase lists free and paid plans with a project/compute cost model.

## Anchored observations (paraphrased)

### Pro; Configure compute for your projects
Pro is listed at USD 25 per month. Paid plans include USD 10 of monthly compute credits, sufficient for one Micro instance. The page lists additional project/compute and usage costs; USD 25 is not an unconditional total bill or spending cap.

### Pro included features
The listed Pro inclusions include daily database backups with seven-day retention. This is hosted database recovery, not a backup of unrelated browser-local application data.
