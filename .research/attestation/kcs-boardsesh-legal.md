# Boardsesh LEGAL.md at repo commit 3340422a27c42e576207ada906c34a5c0c5ab4be (last-updated line in file: 10-08-2026)

## Key passages (verbatim)
- Repo code: "Boardsesh's code is open source under the Apache 2.0 licence. That licence covers the code. It does not cover the hosted service: the accounts, climb database, logbook sync and real-time servers that the Boardsesh apps and website connect to."
- "The public API documented on the API docs page is for integrations: tools that read climb data, or act on behalf of a signed-in climber from their own account, within the rate limits the service applies."
- "The hosted service is not a backend for other apps. A publicly distributed app or fork that uses Boardsesh accounts, data or servers as its service needs our written permission, and we do not currently grant it."
- "You do not have to start from an empty database either. The nightly board snapshots are public SQLite files of the climb catalogs, cheap for us to serve, and you are welcome to use them. We are also happy to talk about syncing non-user data such as climbs and grades to an alternative service."
- Position on data: climbs are "factual, functional" data (cites Feist); databases are "almost entirely user-generated content"; summarizes Aurora Terms as granting Aurora a "perpetual, unrestricted, unlimited, non-exclusive, irrevocable license" over Contributed Content (this is Boardsesh's characterization of Aurora's terms, not a fetched Aurora source). Positions are Boardsesh's own legal stance, not legal advice (file says so).
- Attribution: "Where climb creator information is available, we attribute the creator by their username." Takedown contact legal@boardsesh.com.
- Contact: legal@boardsesh.com.
