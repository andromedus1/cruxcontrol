# Kilter 'Get Your Data Back' claim page

## Summary
Official data-recovery flow for the pre-March-2026 (Aurora-operated) Kilter app.

## Key passages (as returned)
- Primary: request export from Aurora support (support@auroraclimbing.com) with old username/email; upload the JSON (up to 6MB) with new account email + new username; processing 5-7 business days. Recovers "climbs, logbook, drafts etc".
- Manual claim (no JSON): recovers only "published climbs (boulders you created)"; "Your logbook, drafts, and other data cannot be recovered this way." Needs old username, approximate climb count, climb names, optional social handle.
- Footer links: Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Product Terms & Conditions.
Scope: per-user ownership/data attachment; not a bulk catalog export.
