# Android Auto Backup

## Anchored observations

**Backup size, schedule and restore:** Auto Backup allows 25 MB per app user. Its schedule requires user-enabled backup, at least twenty-four hours since the prior backup, an idle device and an eligible network. Applications can configure inclusion or opt out. Data is restored after app installation and before the user can launch it.
