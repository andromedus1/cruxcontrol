# Electron — Introduction

## Anchored observations (paraphrased)

### What is Electron?

Electron embeds Chromium and Node.js for JavaScript/HTML/CSS desktop applications on Windows, macOS and Linux. The stated supported targets are desktop operating systems, not iOS or Android.
