# R2 pricing

## Anchored observations

**Free tier and standard storage:** The Standard free tier lists 10 GB-month of storage, one million Class A requests and ten million Class B requests monthly, with free direct egress. Infrequent Access is excluded and has additional retrieval/minimum-duration rules. Storage uses averaged daily peaks for GB-month accounting.
