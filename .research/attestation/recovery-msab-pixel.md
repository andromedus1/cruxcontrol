# MSAB XRY 11.0.1 announcement

## Summary

MSAB advertises a Pixel full-filesystem acquisition capability in XRY Pro.

## Anchored observations

- **Full File System Access for Pixel Devices:** names Google Pixel 6 and Pixel 8
  and use of their Generic profiles for the new extraction solution.
- **Page footer:** publication date is May 14, 2025; author is MSAB.
- **Release notes paragraph:** directs readers to the customer portal for detailed
  release notes. The public Pixel paragraph gives no Android version, security-patch
  range, recovery probability or guarantee about deleted IndexedDB records.

Vendor capability claims are not an independent device validation.
