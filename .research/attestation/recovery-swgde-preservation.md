# SWGDE mobile evidence preservation and acquisition

## Summary

SWGDE describes state-dependent preservation and differentiates acquisition types.

## Anchored observations

- **Section 5:** unnecessary manual access can alter data; access should have a
  specific preservation/acquisition purpose.
- **Device - ON / Keep Screen Active and Maintain Power:** powered-on devices need
  state preservation and power. The guidance seeks an active screen for an unlocked
  device. The Device - ON discussion warns that an inactivity-triggered reboot can
  move a device from after-first-unlock to before-first-unlock and reduce available
  data. It does not establish an individual device's current timer configuration.
- **Section 5.4.1:** devices received powered off should remain off pending assessment.
- **Section 6, acquisition definitions:** full filesystem collects available active
  files/folders; filesystem acquisitions may contain deleted remnants. These are not
  promises of recovery of every deleted file.

The document is general forensic guidance; device-specific procedures can differ.
