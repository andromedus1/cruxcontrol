# R2 bucket locks

## Anchored observations

**Bucket lock rules and notes:** Lock rules prevent deletion and overwriting for a chosen age, date or indefinite period. Prefix rules can cover existing and new objects; the longest applicable retention wins and overrides earlier lifecycle deletion. Operators can remove lock rules through the control plane.
