# Supabase — Project Pausing

## Summary

Free projects can pause for low database activity; paid projects avoid inactivity pausing.

## Anchored observations (paraphrased)

### How automatic pausing works
Free projects with insufficient database activity over a seven-day period can be paused. Low activity, not strictly zero activity, is the criterion. The owner receives warning and confirmation messages.

### Preventing automatic project pausing
Paid projects are not subject to inactivity pausing. The guide recommends upgrading to Pro to avoid this behavior. Paused free projects can be resumed from the dashboard within the stated restoration window.
