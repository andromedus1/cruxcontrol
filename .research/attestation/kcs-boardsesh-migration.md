# Boardsesh: Migrate from Old Kilter App

## Summary
Aurora Kilter backend shut down March 25 (per page; other sources say Mar 26). Steps: email Aurora for JSON (ascents, attempts, circuits), create Boardsesh account, import. Kilter 'released their own app, still in beta'. Boardsesh: 'open-source alternative to all board climbing apps' with its own climb database copies, self-hostable, will provide data checkout. No mention of Kilter's claim flow, IDs, or Kilter's stance on third parties.
