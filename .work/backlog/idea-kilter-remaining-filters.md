# Remaining Kilter filter parity

Andrew wants the managed Kilter inventory to support everything the Kilter app
can filter by. On 2026-10-18 he chose statistics filters first (grade range, rating,
send count, setter and sorting), owned by `kilter-catalog-filters`, and deferred
picking specific holds on the board. Retain hold selection and the remaining parity
comparison for later. Personal sent/liked filters require data absent from the
anonymous installed catalog; the local logbook remains separately deferred.
