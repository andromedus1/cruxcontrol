Before CruxControl is publicly distributed, resolve whether Kilter-sourced hold and
board imagery may ship with the app. It is acceptable to use the supplied reference
image during private/local prototyping. Before release, either obtain permission,
replace it with imagery Andrew owns and can redistribute, or create sufficiently
original artwork. Do not let prototype asset use silently become a public-release
assumption.
